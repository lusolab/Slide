Bidder Terms & Conditions
1. Eligibility

Open to individuals aged 18 and above holding a valid NRIC or passport, and to registered dealers or companies verified via SSM records.

2. Bidding conduct

Bids placed cannot be retracted. There is no limit to the number of bids you may place, but you must be able to complete payment for any lot you win.

3. Fees

A buyer's premium of 8% of the hammer price applies to all won lots, in addition to applicable shipping and insurance charges.

4. Payment

Winning bidders must complete payment within 3 business days. Accepted methods are local credit/debit cards and bank transfer.

5. Authenticity

Every lot is inspected by a certified horologist prior to listing. Full condition reports are available on each lot page.

6. Disputes

TimeBid reserves the right to withhold or postpone confirmation of a winning bid pending investigation of any dispute between bidders.
