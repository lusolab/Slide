⭐ Reputation system — review score (good to have)
A reputation layer that rewards consistent fast service and punishes sloppy play. Each player has their own independent review score — teammate performance does not affect yours. Designed to add a second axis of competition beyond raw income, especially meaningful in 2v2 where one player can hold strong reputation while their partner recovers.
Review scoreStarsCustomer spawn effectWhat triggers change
5 — Excellent⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐Normal spawn rateServing 3 happy customers in a row
4 — Good⭐⭐⭐⭐Normal spawn rate1 sad or leaving customer
3 — Average⭐⭐⭐-10% spawn rate2–3 sad/leaving customers
2 — Poor⭐⭐-15% spawn rate4–5 sad/leaving customers
1 — Terrible⭐-20% spawn rate · leftover milk risk6+ sad/leaving customers
⚠️ At review score 1, customers arrive 20% less frequently — meaning any milk you've already produced may sit unsold and go to waste as leftover stock. This compounds the punishment: slower income AND wasted resources. Players must serve 3 happy customers consecutively to recover one star.
Why it works for 2v2
Creates a second leaderboard dimension beyond money. A team ahead on cash but bleeding reputation gives the other team a real comeback path. Keeps both teams engaged until the final minute.
Risk to watch
If the penalty hits too early it can snowball and feel unfair. Recommend: review score only starts tracking after the first 3 minutes so new players aren't punished during the learning curve.
Recovery mechanic
3 consecutive happy serves = +1 star. This gives players a clear goal to chase and prevents permanent punishment. Speed cards become extra valuable when reputation is low.
Leftover milk
At review 1, slower spawns mean milk produced sits in the collection pad longer. Players who over-produce premium milk may waste expensive batches — a strategic risk to manage.
