Rollout Plan
Phased implementation for low risk, high impact
A three-phase approach that starts with quick wins and scales to full nationwide deployment.
P1

Phase 1 — Pilot (Months 1–4)

Deploy at 3 high-traffic R&R stops and 2 KTM stations. Install kiosks, smart toilet sensors, and digital screens. Collect baseline data and user feedback.

P2

Phase 2 — Expand (Months 5–10)

Roll out to 15 additional locations. Launch QR rewards programme with merchant partners. Activate programmatic ad network and refine based on Phase 1 learnings.

P3

Phase 3 — Scale (Months 11–18)

Nationwide deployment across all major R&R stops and KTM stations. Full data analytics dashboard for operators. Tourism board integration and cross-border partnerships.
